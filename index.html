/about-me

Hello to whoever is reading this! I'm Nicholas, but you can call me Nico or Nick for short - a structural engineer turned software developer.

I spent my degree and a year in industry designing buildings, working out how to make buildings and structures stand up safely within real-life constraints.

I enjoyed problem solving the most, so I followed it into code where my passion truly lies. I'm now currently doing an MSc in Computer Science (AI) at Nottingham and building my skills on the side.

The engineering background didn't get left behind, it's helped me how I think. I like breaking messy problems into smaller problems, sweating the details, and seeing something through to a finished product.

I'm currently working through refining my web development fundamentals and building this site as I go.

Away from the keyboard, I love to stay active - whether that's on a basketball court 🏀 in a boxing gym 🥊 outside for a run 🏃 or in the gym training 🏋.
